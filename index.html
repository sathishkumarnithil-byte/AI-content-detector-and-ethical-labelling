How was it made?
Detection is probabilistic. No tool, this one included, can prove who or what wrote a text, and these signals also appear in human writing, especially from non-native English writers and formal styles. Do not use this score to accuse anyone. Self-disclosure with a label is the more reliable and fairer practice.
